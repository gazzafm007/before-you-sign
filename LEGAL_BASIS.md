# Before You Sign — legal statements the checker relies on

These are the only legal rules the checker (`lib/prompt.dart`, version 0.4) may rely on when it says a document **conflicts with the law**. Each needs ARPRA to confirm the reference before the public release. Where a statement is marked **[confirm]** in the prompt, the app only says "may conflict with".

Sources:
- *Residential (Land Lease) Communities Act 2013* (NSW), the version in force from 25 September 2024. The 2024 amending Act commenced in two stages: most provisions on 25.9.2024, and ss 83–84 on 11.12.2024. No later amendments are listed.
- The prescribed standard form of site agreement (Regulation 2015, Sch 1), as amended by the *Amendment Regulation 2024* (2024 No 415): 25.9.2024, with standard terms 9.4–9.5 from 11.12.2024.
- The Australian Consumer Law (ACL).

Status key:
- **Act text**: wording read in the Act.
- **Form**: wording read in the prescribed form.
- **Heading only**: section heading seen, operative words not yet read.
- **Inference**: ARPRA's reading, with no express words found.

## How the checker uses these

Every red and amber item now carries a **basis**, shown on the report:

- **Against the law · [reference]**: conflicts with L1–L14. If the statement is marked [confirm] or is an inference, it says "may conflict with".
- **May be an unfair term · ACL ss 23–24**: L15. It never says a term *is* unfair.
- **Lawful – worth knowing**: costs, limits on what the buyer can do, or one-sided terms. No legal claim.
- **Missing or unclear**: blanks, tick-boxes, contradictions, ambiguous drafting.

Language rules: no "illegal", "unlawful", "excessive", "high", "unreasonable" or "unfair" as the checker's own conclusion. Fee amounts are shown as figures and projections, never judged.

## Effect on the Stewart test (Archer's Run, documents of July 2024, advice 13 August 2024)

The 2024 form changes commenced on 25 September 2024. For a check as at 13 August 2024, the following findings in the first report reflect later law, not errors in the documents:
- missing 16A, 21A and 28.3 testing
- 9.4–9.5 wording
- termination Note 2(g)

Note 2(g) was in the form until the 2024 amendment removed it. Findings that do not depend on the date still stand: "select ALL THAT APPLY", the increase wording, the 13 32 30 footer, the blanks and unticked boxes, and the additional terms.

Whether clause 16's "or your home" differed from the pre-September 2024 form needs checking against the earlier form.
