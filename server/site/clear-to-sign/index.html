Clear to Sign by ARPRA

A free app that checks your NSW land lease community paperwork and gives you a plain-English summary of what to look at before you sign.

What it does

  • Scan the Disclosure Statement, Residential Site Agreement, Community Rules and the contract for the home with your phone, or add PDF files.
  • The app checks them against the Residential (Land Lease) Communities Act 2013 and the standard form site agreement.
  • You get a free summary: an overall rating, the things that matter most, key dates, and anything that may conflict with the law.
  • If you want a person to look, you can ask for ARPRA's Prepurchase Advisory review. An ARPRA adviser reads your documents and the report and gives you written advice. The review is paid by card on a secure Stripe page and includes 12 months' ARPRA membership.

How to use it

  1. Tap Start a new check and add each document. Photograph every page, in order, or add the PDF.
  2. Read how your documents are checked and tick the box to agree.
  3. Wait two to four minutes for your report. Keep the app open.
  4. Read the summary. Tap Get ARPRA to review this if you want an adviser to check it.

Important

Clear to Sign is an automated first check, not legal or financial advice. It can miss things or misread a page. Talk to ARPRA, a solicitor or NSW Fair Trading before you sign.
