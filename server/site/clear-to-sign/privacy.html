Clear to Sign — privacy notice

How the Clear to Sign app and ARPRA handle your documents and details. This notice adds to ARPRA's privacy policy.

ARPRA Limited, PO Box 4063, Forster NSW 2428. Last updated 27 November 2026.

What we collect

  • Your documents: the pages you scan or add (photos or PDF files). They may include names, addresses and other personal details, such as yours or the operator's.
  • Optional details you type: the community name, site number and notes.
  • A random device code that the app creates, so you can follow your check and so we can limit the number of free checks. It does not identify you.
  • If you ask for a review: your name, email address, phone number and when you plan to sign.

The app has no accounts, advertising or tracking, and does not use the advertising ID.

How we use it

  • To prepare your report.
  • If you ask for ARPRA's review: to provide that service, contact you and send you the full report.
  • To keep the service working and to prevent misuse (for example, daily limits on free checks).

Who it goes to

  • ARPRA's server, hosted on Microsoft Azure in Australia (Australia East).
  • Anthropic, an artificial intelligence (AI) company in the United States. Its AI model, Claude, reads your documents to prepare the report. We ask your permission in the app before anything is sent. Anthropic does not use your documents to train its AI.
  • Stripe, if you pay for a review. You enter your card on Stripe's secure page; ARPRA never sees or stores your card details.
  • Microsoft 365, which ARPRA uses for email, if you ask for a review.

Anthropic and Stripe may handle information outside Australia, including in the United States. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Documents from a free check are deleted from our server after 7 days.
  • The check report is kept for 90 days, in case you ask us to review it, then deleted from the server.
  • If you ask for a review, your documents, report and contact details are sent to our Prepurchase Advisory team and kept with your advice file, as for any ARPRA advice.
  • Reports you save on your phone stay there until you delete them or remove the app.

Security

Everything sent between the app and ARPRA is encrypted. The key to the AI service is kept on ARPRA's server, never on your phone.

Your choices

  • You can choose not to agree, and use ARPRA's Prepurchase Advisory service without the AI check by calling 1300 798 399.
  • You can withdraw your agreement in the app under Settings and help.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing prepurchase@arpra.org.au with your reference.

Questions and complaints

Contact ARPRA on 1300 798 399 or prepurchase@arpra.org.au. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
